Calculation: Estimated stock is opening stock at the start of its date, plus recorded receipts converted to consumption units, less logged quantities taken from that date onward. Same-day receipts are added; do not include them in the opening count. Complete days of stock is estimated stock divided by daily dosage, rounded down; it is unavailable when the estimate is negative or the inputs are unavailable. The reorder threshold is daily dosage × (lead time + one day); the separately entered reorder level is not used. Suggested orders target 30 days of supply after delivery: 30-day demand plus lead-time demand, less estimated stock, rounded up to whole order units.

Limitations: Estimates exclude unrecorded receipts and purchases, adjustments and unlogged consumption; repeated suggestions do not account for outstanding orders. A negative balance indicates a discrepancy and should be checked. Older logs without a recorded quantity and unit make the estimate unavailable; edit the log only if the actual dose is known. System lead time requires linked order and receipt history. Medicines are matched by name, not formulation or strength; doses from different practitioners are summed. Items without a current prescription are grouped separately. Verify prescriptions and physical stock. Prototype only; not for clinical use.
