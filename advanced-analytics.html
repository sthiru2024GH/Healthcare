Recorded parameters

Reading the charts: Each parameter uses its own vertical scale based on its readings, and its own unit. The scale remains visible while you scroll; reference ranges are not shown here. As in Lab Test Trend, each chart shows only that parameter's recorded dates, equally spaced even when the time between tests differs. Dates and scroll positions may differ between charts; check the date labels when comparing them. Lines connect recorded readings rather than showing measurements between them. Different slopes do not establish a relationship or cause. Review results with a practitioner. Prototype only; not for clinical use.
